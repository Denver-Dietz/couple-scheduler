## 2026-09-23 - Schedule Panels Accessibility Enhancements\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like Trash2 or X) are frequently used in repeated lists (like shifts or schedules) but often lack `aria-label` attributes, rendering them invisible to screen readers in this specific app.\n**Action:** Always proactively search for `<button><Icon /></button>` patterns without text content when creating or auditing list-based UI components, and add descriptive `aria-label`s.
